About Us

Who We Are

Setu — meaning "bridge" — is exactly what we build: a vital link in the polymer value chain.

Setu Global redirects high-value post-industrial plastic waste away from landfills and back into production lines, working with manufacturers, recyclers, and traders across borders to keep material moving in a closed loop.

We work at the intersection of trade and sustainability, sourcing consistent volumes of film, scrap, and resin and delivering them where manufacturing needs them most.

Our Mission

To make plastic manufacturing sustainable by supplying secondary plastic raw materials with transparency, speed, and standard quality checks — at every port, every time.
