Founder

A background in law and policy, now applied to the realities of global trade.

Founder and Proprietor

Setu Global

Five years at GNLU, followed by a career in policy consulting, built a world that was entirely about law and regulation. It was comfortable, intellectual, and expected. That path was set aside to build Setu Global instead, a venture handling international trade and logistics for plastic and polymer raw materials, bridging the gap between global supply and domestic manufacturing.

The transition from policy drafts to the day to day reality of global shipping was not an easy one. The learning curve was steep, and there were days that felt genuinely overwhelming, along with real moments of doubt about trading a predictable career for the unpredictability of supply chains. Through that, Setu Global has already completed multiple consignments successfully across the globe.

In hindsight, the years in law and policy were not a detour. International trade is, at its core, a web of compliance, regulation, and risk management, and that analytical foundation is what keeps the business moving forward today.

Every transaction at Setu Global is backed by transparent documentation: clear Bills of Lading, precise specifications, and timely settlements, the same discipline that governance and compliance work demands, now applied to building long term domestic and international partnerships.
