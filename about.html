About Us

The bridge between global supply and domestic manufacturing.

Setu, meaning bridge, is exactly what we build. We connect polymer supply across borders with the manufacturing units that need it.

Who We Are

A vital link in the polymer value chain

Setu Global redirects high value post industrial plastic waste away from landfills and back into production lines, working with manufacturers, recyclers, and traders across borders to keep material moving in a closed loop.

We work at the intersection of trade and sustainability, sourcing consistent volumes of film, scrap, and resin and delivering them where manufacturing needs them most.

We are also extremely competitive in our pricing. We believe fair, transparent rates build the kind of long term partnerships that this industry runs on.

Our Mission

To make plastic manufacturing sustainable by supplying secondary plastic raw materials with transparency, speed, and standard quality checks, at every port, every time.

Global Footprint

Trade routes and key hubs

A growing network of origin hubs and destination ports spanning multiple continents.

  • Origin HubsUSA, Canada, UAE and Europe
  • Destination PortsWe deliver across the globe, to any port

End to End Handling

We manage the entire consignment ourselves, from picking up the material at origin to delivering it to the doorstep of your unit. Logistics is handled in house, not outsourced, so you deal with one team from start to finish.

Extremely Competitive Pricing

Our rates are built to be among the most competitive in the trade, without cutting corners on grading, documentation, or delivery timelines.
